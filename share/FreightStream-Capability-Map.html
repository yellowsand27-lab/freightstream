不要用「关单天数」考核Do not use days-to-close as a KPI

全库平均 340 天才关单、89% 超过 60 天——说明关单是财务批量动作，不是操作时效。拿它做 KPI 会考出一堆假问题，还会逼员工去刷一个没有业务含义的指标。应该用开票延迟和费用入账延迟，那才是操作员真正能控制的。JoannaW、ClaraL 的低结案率同理：先问是不是角色不同，再谈绩效。

Files close after 340 days on average and 89% take over 60 days — closing is a finance batch action, not a measure of operational speed. As a KPI it manufactures false problems and pushes staff to game a number that means nothing. Use billing lag and cost-booking lag instead; those are what an operator actually controls. Same caution for JoannaW and ClaraL: ask whether their role differs before treating it as performance.

先分流，再分析Segment first, then analyse

2024 年起应收里，按提单开票的直客业务占 57%，按周期批量结算的代理业务占 43%。单票盈利、航线毛利、客户毛利、滞期回收率——这些指标在代理业务上全都不成立。两套业务必须分成两套口径，混在同一张报表里必错。这条要作为强制维度写进数据层，而不是靠分析师每次记得。

Of receivables since 2024, direct customers billed per file account for 57% and agency business billed per settlement cycle for 43%. Per-file profitability, lane margin, customer margin, demurrage recovery — none of these hold for the agency segment. The two need two separate definitions; mixed into one report they will be wrong. Enforce it as a dimension in the data layer rather than relying on an analyst to remember.

07 — 建议07 — Recommendation

先交付开票及时性看板Ship the billing timeliness board first

它见效最快、口径最干净、而且能直接换成现金。做完这一项，管理层就能看到这套系统的价值， 后面几项的推进阻力会小很多。同时把「直客 / 代理」的口径分流写进数据层——这是所有后续指标正确性的前提。

It delivers fastest, rests on the cleanest definitions, and converts directly into cash. Once it lands, management can see what this system is worth, and the remaining items will meet far less resistance. Alongside it, write the direct-versus-agency split into the data layer — every downstream metric depends on it.

第七项（合规与提柜防范）建议同步启动，但走的是业务流程改造，不是分析开发。它的前置条件是让操作把提柜时间、 免费期、ISF 报送时间录进系统，这件事越早开始，能用的历史数据就越多。

Item seven (compliance and pickup prevention) should start in parallel, but as a business process change rather than analytics development. It depends on operators recording pickup times, last free days and ISF filing times. The earlier that starts, the more history there is to work with.
